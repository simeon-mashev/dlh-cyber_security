## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the acceptable use of company information technology resources, including networks, systems, devices, email, internet, and collaboration tools. The objective is to ensure that all technology resources are used responsibly, legally, and in alignment with the business goals of TechSecure Solutions Inc., while protecting company data, infrastructure, and reputation from misuse.

---

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Applicability

This policy applies to:

- [x] All employees
- [x] Contractors and consultants
- [x] Third-party vendors
- [x] Remote workers and telecommuters
- [x] Any individual with access to company systems or data

### 2.2 Systems/Assets Covered

- Corporate email system
- Cloud-based development environment
- VPN and remote access systems
- Collaboration tools (chat, video conferencing, document sharing)
- Company-issued desktops, laptops, and mobile devices
- Personal devices connected to the corporate network (BYOD)
- Corporate network and internet connection
- All software installed on company systems
- All data stored, processed, or transmitted using company resources

### 2.3 Exclusions

- This policy does not apply to personal devices that are not used to access company resources or connect to the corporate network.
- Employees may use personal devices for purely personal activities outside of work hours, provided such activities do not involve company data or systems.

## 3. Policy Statements

### 3.1 Internet Usage

Employees must use company internet access primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work duties or consume excessive bandwidth.

Requirements:

- Personal internet use must not involve illegal activities, gambling, pornography, hate speech, or other offensive content.
- Streaming of audio/video for non-work purposes is discouraged during business hours unless approved by a manager.
- Access to social media for personal reasons is allowed only during breaks or outside working hours, except when required for business tasks.
- All internet traffic may be monitored and logged for security and compliance purposes. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company networks.
- Downloading or accessing pirated software, copyrighted material without permission, or malicious websites is strictly prohibited.

### 3.2 Email Usage

Corporate email is provided for business communication. Personal use is permitted on a limited basis but must comply with this policy.

Requirements:

- Employees must not use company email for sending unsolicited bulk messages, chain letters, or offensive content.
- Sending confidential company information to personal email accounts is prohibited unless explicitly approved for remote work or backup.
- Email retention: Important business emails must be retained according to the company’s retention schedule (see Data Handling policy). Deletion of emails related to ongoing legal or regulatory matters is not allowed.
- Employees must not open email attachments or click links from unknown or suspicious senders. Report any phishing attempts to the IT Security Team.
- Use of email for harassment, discrimination, or any activity that creates a hostile work environment is strictly forbidden.
- Automatic forwarding of company email to personal accounts is prohibited unless approved by IT and management.

### 3.3 Software

Only authorized software may be installed on company systems. All software installations must follow the formal approval process.

Requirements:

- Employees must not install unapproved software on company-issued devices. This includes freeware, shareware, and trial versions.
- Requests for new software must be submitted to the IT department and approved by the employee’s manager and IT Security Team.
- All software must be properly licensed. Use of pirated or unlicensed software is a violation of this policy and may result in legal action.
- Software updates and security patches must be applied promptly as directed by IT.
- Cloud-based applications that access company data must be approved and inventoried by IT.
- Personal software may not be installed on company devices. If an employee needs a personal tool, they must use a personal device (if permitted under BYOD).

### 3.4 Social Media

Employees must be careful when using social media, especially when discussing the company, its projects, clients, or employees.

Requirements:

- Confidential company information must never be posted on social media. This includes project details, client names, financial data, source code, and internal communications.
- Employees must not represent the company on social media without explicit authorization. Only designated spokespersons or marketing personnel may speak on behalf of the company.
- Personal social media posts should not contain the company name or imply endorsement without permission.
- Employees must not post defamatory or disparaging comments about the company, colleagues, or clients. Such posts may be considered a violation of this policy and may result in disciplinary action.
- If an employee identifies themselves as a TechSecure Solutions employee on social media, their posts must state that views are personal and not company positions.
- Social media activity that affects the company’s reputation or violates confidentiality is subject to investigation and enforcement.

### 3.5 BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)

Employees may use personal devices (smartphones, tablets, laptops) to access company resources, subject to registration and security requirements.

Requirements:

- All personal devices that connect to the corporate network or access company data must be registered with the IT department before use.
- Personal devices must have up-to-date operating systems and security software (antivirus, firewall) as required by IT.
- Devices must be protected with a strong passcode or biometric lock.
- Remote wipe capability must be enabled if the device is lost or stolen. IT may initiate a remote wipe of company data at any time.
- Company data on personal devices must be separated from personal data. Use of containerization or separate work profiles is recommended.
- Personal devices are not permitted to store sensitive company data unless explicitly approved and encrypted.
- The company reserves the right to revoke BYOD access at any time for security reasons.
- Employees must report lost or stolen devices immediately to the IT Security Team.

### 3.6 Data Handling

All company data must be handled according to its classification and sensitivity.

Requirements:

- Data must be stored only on approved company systems or approved cloud services. Storing sensitive data on personal devices or external drives is prohibited.
- Sharing of data with third parties must be authorized and logged. Use company-approved sharing mechanisms (e.g., secure file transfer, collaboration platforms).
- Data must be disposed of securely when no longer needed. Paper records must be shredded; electronic media must be wiped or destroyed following IT guidelines.
- Employees must not email sensitive data to personal accounts or use non-approved file-sharing services.
- Data backups are the responsibility of IT; employees should not create duplicate sensitive data sets on local drives.
- All data breaches or suspected data leaks must be reported immediately to the IT Security Team.

## 5. Compliance

### 5.1 Monitoring

Compliance is monitored through:

- Regular review of internet and email usage logs
- Network monitoring for unauthorized devices or software
- Automated alerts for suspicious activity
- Periodic audits of BYOD device registration and security posture

### 5.2 Reporting

The IT Security Team will provide a quarterly compliance report to the CISO, including:

- Number of policy violations detected
- Number of unauthorized software installations
- Number of BYOD devices out of compliance
- Results of any audits

### 5.3 Auditing

An independent audit of AUP compliance will be conducted annually, or more frequently if required by regulatory or business changes. Audit results will be documented and reviewed by management.

---

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Verbal warning
- Written warning
- Suspension of access privileges
- Disciplinary action up to and including termination
- Legal action where applicable

### 6.2 Reporting Violations

Report suspected violations to: **IT Security Team via security(@)techsecure.com or the incident reporting portal**

---

## 7. Exceptions

### 7.1 Exception Process

Exceptions to this policy require:

1. Written request to the CISO or Policy Owner
2. Business justification
3. Risk assessment
4. Compensating controls (if applicable)
5. Formal approval and documentation

### 7.2 Exception Duration

All exceptions must have a defined end date and be reviewed quarterly. Exceptions may be extended only by re-approval.
