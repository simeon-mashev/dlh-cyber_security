# Severity in CVE

CVEs can be categorised into different severity levels based off the CVSS (Common Vulnerability Scoring System) score. This allows companies and organizations to prioritise the vulnerabilities with higher severity.

A **Critical** vulnerability has the highest severity, it must be patched or mitigated immediately to avoid risking major disruptions and system compromise. These types of vulnerabilities are easy to exploit and can cause the most amount of damage if exploited.

A vulnerability with a **High** severity usually either has a lower impact if exploited and/or is harder to exploit when compared to a critical severity vulnerability. It it still must be patched or mitigated, though it is not as much of an immediate concern as a critical one.

A **Medium** severity vulnerability will either require a very complex attack with many steps for successful exploitation and/or will have a relatively limited impact compared to the 2 severities above it.

A **Low** severity vulnerability will have minimal impact and have the lowest priority for patching and mitigation. Usually them being exploited will not affect the company in any meaningful way.
