# Analyzing Vulnerability Trends

If you look up Linux kernel in the nvd website, the statistics show a huge jump in reported vulnerabilities from 2024 onwards, possibly due to the rise of AI vulnerability scanning. The number in 2026 has already surpassed the nubmer from 2024, and we are still in August. Using the advanced filters we can also see that in 2026 there have been a lot more published CVEs related to the Linux kernel in Q2 compared to Q1. 633 pages of results for Q1 and 1966 pages of results for Q2.
